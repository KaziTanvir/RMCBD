Ask about your circumstances ↗
What accounting services can a CA firm provide in Bangladesh?+

Depending on its professional authorisation and agreed engagement scope, a CA firm can provide audit and assurance, bookkeeping, financial reporting, income-tax assistance and business advisory. VAT and company-filing support should be discussed against your specific requirements.

Can you help with income tax, e-TIN and VAT/BIN matters?+

We can discuss income-tax return preparation, e-TIN assistance, VAT registration or BIN documentation, and recordkeeping needs. The work depends on your business activities, taxpayer status and current NBR requirements. Applicable obligations are reviewed for each engagement.

What should I prepare for an initial consultation?+

Bring a short description of your business, the service you need and a summary of your existing records. Relevant documents may include financial statements, previous returns and registration details. Agree a secure document-sharing method before sending confidential information.

Do you support company incorporation and RJSC returns?+

Company support may include preparing documentation for name clearance, incorporation and return filing. The exact scope depends on your company structure and filing requirements. RJSC is the official authority for these company services.

How are fees and delivery dates agreed?+

Fees and delivery dates depend on the work required, record quality and engagement scope. Request a written proposal covering deliverables, required documents, fees and a timetable before work begins.
